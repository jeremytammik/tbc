Autodesk TBC Archive

Autodesk published another archive incarnation of The Building Coder up until August 2025.

It is part of the new home at blog.autodesk.io for multiple blogs authored by members of the APS team on Typepad until its shutdown in August 2025. Refer to About Us for a list of all the original blog names and URLs.

Just like the preceding ones, the new mirror site is based on The Building Coder source code on GitHub. Here are the mirrors I am aware of so far:

  • The Building Coder on ArchiLabs.ai by ArchiLabs.ai
  • The Building Coder on AecWithCode.com by Tomo Sugeta
  • The Building Coder on blog.autodesk.io by Autodesk

Thank you Autodesk for sharing a new TBC archive mirror.

That said, I hope to continue publishing new material here in the live blog, The Building Coder on GitHub.
