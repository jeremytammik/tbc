### Autodesk TBC Archive

Autodesk published another archive incarnation 
of [The Building Coder up until August 2025](https://blog.autodesk.io/category/thebuildingcoder/).

It is part of the new home 
at [blog.autodesk.io](https://blog.autodesk.io) for
multiple blogs authored by members of the APS team on Typepad until its shutdown in August 2025. 
Refer to [About Us](https://blog.autodesk.io/about-us/) for a list of all the original blog names and URLs.

Just like the preceding ones, the new mirror site is based 
on [The Building Coder source code on GitHub](https://github.com/jeremytammik/tbc).
Here are the mirrors I am aware of so far:

- [The Building Coder on ArchiLabs.ai](https://thebuildingcoder.archilabs.ai/) by ArchiLabs.ai
- [The Building Coder on AecWithCode.com](https://tbc.aecwithcode.com/) by Tomo Sugeta
- [The Building Coder on blog.autodesk.io](https://blog.autodesk.io) by Autodesk

Thank you Autodesk for sharing a new TBC archive mirror.

That said, I hope to continue publishing new material here in the live blog,
[The Building Coder on GitHub](https://jeremytammik.github.io/tbc/a/index.html).
